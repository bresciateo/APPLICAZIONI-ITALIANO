The Mystery Classmate

Your mission

Interview a classmate. Write their answers using "I", then change them into sentences with "he" or "she". Keep the name secret for the class game!

Seleziona le attività. La domanda è un modello: puoi ascoltarla o leggerla al compagno. Puoi anche inventarne una tua.

Ask, listen and write

Prima trascrivi la risposta in prima persona (I + avverbio a scelta), poi riscrivila come descrizione del compagno. Usa Check my work! per conservare una versione.

Invent Your Monster!

Your mission

Invent a monster! Describe how it looks, then imagine its habits. Exchange your descriptions and drawings with your classmates.

Part 1 · Describe your monster

Describe its appearance. Then give your description to a classmate who will draw it.

Part 2 · Give your monster a life!

Now describe its habits. Your classmate can add new details to the drawing.

Part 3 · Bring a monster to life!

Look at a classmate's drawing. Describe what you can see and imagine new habits. Your ideas don't have to match the first writer's ideas.
